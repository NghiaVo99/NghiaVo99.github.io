Nghia Van Vo

Ph.D. student in Applied Mathematics, Oakland University
Advised by Professor Tran T. A. Nghia

I design fast Newton-type algorithms for nonsmooth optimization, and I study when solutions of regularized inverse problems can be trusted under noise. My tools come from variational analysis and generalized differentiation.

I expect to complete my Ph.D. in May 2027 and am applying for tenure-track and postdoctoral positions in mathematics.
