We develop inexact adaptive algorithms for minimizing difference-of-convex (DC) functions in Hilbert spaces. The proposed method, I-ADCA, allows inexact subgradients and inexact solutions of the convex subproblems while keeping global convergence to stationary points, and it admits explicit convergence rates under a Polyak–Łojasiewicz-type property. The main application is elliptic optimal control with control constraints and a nonconvex $$L^1-L^2$$ sparsity-promoting penalty; combined with finite element discretization, this gives an efficient solution procedure with proven well-posedness and error bounds, confirmed by numerical experiments.
